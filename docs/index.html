Custody is architecture

The model may ask.
It may not dispose.

A convincing response is not a safety boundary. The tool contract is.

The model may chooseA known catalog item, a channel response, and when to request a read.
The model cannot chooseMerchant, mint, decimals, price, replay window, finality, device authority, GPIO pin, pulse length or signing key.
The watcher provesA successful finalized transaction credited the configured merchant in the configured asset and amount.
The watcher does not proveThat a motor moved or an item arrived. That belongs to the actuator journal and delivery sensor.
The agent holdsPublic addresses and, optionally, an encrypted RPC credential supplied through config_read.
The agent never holdsCustomer, merchant, device or nonce-authority private keys.
